Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app → tap the profile icon → Subscription → "Restore Purchases". Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not your device. You can restore it on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Can I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not IT-Muscle. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID to request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled for IT-Muscle Fitness (Settings → Your Name → iCloud → IT-Muscle Fitness), your data will be included in your iCloud backup and restored when you reinstall the app.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. Your purchase is retained by Apple and can be restored after reinstalling. If iCloud Backup was enabled, your workout and nutrition data can also be restored.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — almost everything works offline. Food barcode scanning and the food search require an internet connection to query the Open Food Facts database. All workout logging, progress tracking, and calculators work fully offline.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Go to the app's Settings tab → Health Integration. You'll be prompted to grant permission. IT-Muscle can read steps, active energy, and heart rate, and write body weight and workout data to Health.

What's the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic ($2.99) unlocks unlimited workout logging and exercise library access. Pro ($9.99) includes everything in Basic plus Apple Health integration, nutrition analytics, and all fitness calculators. Both are one-time purchases — no subscriptions.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@it-muscle.com with your iOS version, app version (found in Settings → About), and a description of what happened. Screenshots are always helpful.
