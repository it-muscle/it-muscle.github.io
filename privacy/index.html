Privacy Policy

IT-Muscle Fitness

Effective: September 8, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 25, 2026

Short version: IT-Muscle stores all your personal data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts and no server-side data storage.

Information We Collect

Data You Enter

All data you enter — food logs, workout sessions, body measurements, weight entries, progress photos, and settings — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Data We Do NOT Collect

✓No name or contact info
✓No user accounts required
✓No usage analytics
✓No advertising SDKs
✓No selling or sharing of your data (service providers below only)
✓No tracking frameworks

Third-Party Services

Open Food Facts

When you search for food or scan a barcode, the app queries the Open Food Facts API — a public food product database. Your search query or scanned barcode is sent to their servers to retrieve nutritional information. No personal identifying information is included. See their Privacy Policy.

Apple HealthKit (Optional — Pro Tier)

If you connect Apple Health, the app:

  • Reads: Step count, active energy burned, and heart rate (for the Home dashboard)
  • Writes: Body weight entries and completed workout sessions

HealthKit data is governed by Apple's privacy policies and your device's Health settings. IT-Muscle does not transmit HealthKit data to any server.

Apple In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store infrastructure. IT-Muscle does not receive or store payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat (purchase processing)

The app uses RevenueCat to verify App Store purchases (Basic, Pro, and the free 7-day trial) and to restore them on your other devices. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your App Store purchase receipts (what you bought and when), and basic device information such as OS and app version. It does not receive your name, email, health data, or anything you log in the app. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Camera and Photo Library

  • Camera: Used for scanning food barcodes and, if you choose, taking progress photos. Progress photos are stored only on your device.
  • Photo Library: Accessed only when you choose to add a progress photo. Photos are stored locally within the app's sandbox.

Notifications

If enabled, local notifications (rest timer alerts, workout reminders) are scheduled entirely on-device. No notification data is sent to a server.

Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored in a local SQLite database within the app's private sandbox. This data is:

  • Backed up by iCloud if you have iCloud Backup enabled for this app (iOS Settings → Your Name → iCloud → IT-Muscle Fitness)
  • Permanently deleted when you delete the app from your device

Children's Privacy

IT-Muscle Fitness is rated 4+ on the App Store. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app complies with COPPA and similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted at it-muscle.github.io/privacy with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
